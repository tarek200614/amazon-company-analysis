Primary/official sources (SEC filings, Amazon's own disclosures, AWS documentation) are prioritized wherever available; secondary sources (research firms, industry press) are used for market-share estimates, competitive benchmarking, and analysis that Amazon does not disclose directly. Every source was verified as active and accurate as of **July 2026**. A formatted APA 7th-edition version of this list is available in [`bibliography.pdf`](./bibliography.pdf).

### Notes on Source Verification

- Primary financial figures (net sales, net income, operating income, segment results) are sourced directly from Amazon's **SEC Form 8-K (Exhibit 99.1)** and **Form 10-K** filings via EDGAR, and cross-checked against the Amazon Investor Relations press release for the same period.
- Cloud market-share estimates (AWS ~28–30%, Azure ~21–25%, Google Cloud ~13–14%) come from **Synergy Research Group's** quarterly hyperscaler tracker; figures are reported as ranges because different secondary aggregators (Statista, DataCenterDynamics, and others) round Synergy's underlying data differently.
- Sustainability figures (2025 carbon footprint, renewable-energy matching, Climate Pledge signatory count) are drawn from **Amazon's own Sustainability Report and disclosures**, corroborated by contemporaneous reporting from GeekWire, Axios, and Bloomberg.
- The **May 13, 2026 Alexa for Shopping / Rufus unification** and **~30,000-role corporate restructuring** are recent developments reported by multiple independent outlets (GeekWire, TechCrunch, Axios) and referenced in Amazon's own announcements.
- Academic and book references provide broader strategic and platform-economics context and are not the source of any specific FY2025 figure.
- Where a statistic could not be verified against a primary Amazon filing (e.g., some employee-productivity or logistics-footprint figures drawn from secondary financial-analysis blogs), the presentation and this repository present it as an estimate and note the secondary source explicitly rather than as an Amazon-disclosed figure.
